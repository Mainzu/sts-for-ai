# Lane final-round: charter

The lane's charter as the navigator wrote it. `lane/final-round` holds a summary and cites this file. It carries the owner's decision "The final training round, with the best setting reached so far" (`decision/final-training-round`) and is the one open lane (`decision/one-lane`).

goal: goal 2 first (the product's next net, at the game's hard setting), goal 1 through the base net later goal-1 work starts from: train the final round at Ascension 20 with the best recipe on record and read it against A20 controls, queued by 2026-11-20 with whatever has landed;

measure, in this order (one step per session where it fits):
1. **A20 readiness (code, now; `decision/a20-default` steps 1-3).** (a) a config key `ascension` (default 0) in `scripts/learn.py`, `scripts/selfplay.py` and `scripts/finetune.py`, written into every fight spec they make (games, labels, validation, self-play rounds); (b) the level recorded in `scripts/registry.py`'s dataset manifest and in run.json, kept apart like the rules version: a dataset or pool that mixes levels is refused unless the config says so; (c) the gauntlet's reward choosers project rests and HP at the run's max HP (75 at A20; `MAX_HP` in `scripts/gauntlet.py`), not a fixed 80. Check: tests that an `ascension: 20` config writes fight specs at 20 and records 20 in the manifest and run.json, that mixed levels are refused, and that a chooser at A20 projects with 75, that an A20 training fight starts with A20's HP and max HP and its deck holds Ascender's Bane (`docs/specs/ascension.md`: the level's `deck_adds`); at A0 every existing gauntlet file replays identically (a short `gauntlet.py play` of the one-turn control on 50 sequences matches its file).
2. **Stall probes (code, then CPU; required before step 5; `decision/final-training-round`, "Prefer the kill").** Backlog 82 (`leaf-beats-win`) and 85 (`stalls`), probed on the real-game records before any control or the run is recorded. Both are fixes to how the search prices a leaf, each behind its own player suffix so nothing changes unless it passes. (a) 82: cap an unfinished leaf's score at the best finish reachable from it (a win at its HP plus the healing its own lines can still add), so no leaf outscores an immediate win at the same HP. Probe: replay the 120 recorded decisions with a winning option not taken (`results/real-game/*/decisions.jsonl`) through the product's search with the cap; pass if it takes the win in the strict cases backlog 82 names (run 56 floor 14, run 52 floor 5, the A0 boss fights 060511, 062806, 080956, 062806 floor 6, 145619 floor 7); kill-fast if it changes none. (b) 85: make progress visible at the leaf, a leaf where no monster lost HP or a defender since the root (or, the history form, one whose monsters repeat their state from earlier in the fight's record at lower player HP) scoring no better than one that did. Probe: replay run 83's floor-5 decisions (`results/real-game/20261114-205554`, turns 6, 20, 40) and the stalls of run 56 (Blue Slaver, turns 7-18) and A0 run 18 (Acid Slime at 4 HP); pass if the search then kills the Shield Gremlin and finishes the other two; kill-fast if it still strikes the block (then the fix needs a net input, backlog 81's, and stays out of this run). Also check how a self-play game that reaches the turn cap ends and is labelled in `scripts/learn.py`: a stalled game labelled like a fight still going would teach the stall, and that is fixed in this step if so. (c) Adoption: the probes that pass go into one arm, one turn deep, on the headline setting at A0 `--against` the one-turn control with `--vary price` under `decision/level-pass` (a misprice fix: level or better passes); on a pass, the product with the fix, two turns deep, `--against` the headline control under the same rule, and on that pass `scripts/product.py set` with the new flags. A combined arm below level reads each fix alone once. A passed fix is then on in this run's self-play search and labels (the learn config's search, wherever it uses the same leaf code) and in both step-5 controls, so the round's read varies only the net. If the gauntlet gains a turns-per-fight field (a few lines in `scripts/gauntlet.py`), step 5's controls and step 7's read report fights over 30 turns by fight type. Cutoff: a fix not passed by 2026-11-20 12:00 stays out; the probes' result is recorded either way. About 10 thread-h (2.4 one turn deep, 7 for the product read).
3. **Seeds-go (read only).** `lane/wide-recipe-confirm` reads seed 101 (jobs 1008-1012) and closes at its seeds-go checkpoint; this lane takes the recipe from that close (`measurement/portfolio-wide-recipe-confirm`): the wide generated recipe (`configs/widepack40k-s*.json`) on a go, else the 40k pack recipe (`configs/packnet40k-s*.json`). If seeds-go is still open on 2026-11-19, the pack recipe goes.
4. **Optional inputs, only while waiting for step 5's engine work** (backlog 78), in order, each landing only with its own test and never delaying step 6: (a) RoundEnd hooks as triggered rows (`cause/roundend-blind`; test: a GE-RoundEnd power writes one triggered row, `series_check` PASS, the product net bit-identical; only if the recipe is the wide one); (b) backlog 81's near-free probe (two-turn leaves with `intent_known` false, and scheduled-minus-averaged monster damage after the leaf against HP lost on Hexaghost replays): the schedule columns join the run only if their probe and burst read pass by 2026-11-19 (81's kill-fast rule). Nothing else is added.
5. **A20 controls (CPU; backlog 80 (a)).** After the owner's Act 1 session lands F, V (with C1 and Shield Gremlin's Protect), C2 and C3 on main and main's `sts` matches main (`HANDOFF-act1-content.md`), or at 2026-11-20 12:00 with whatever has landed: record, pinned to that commit, the one-turn control (`yardstick/one-turn-controls`'s headline command) and the product (`product/net`, `configs/product.json`) on the headline setting with `--ascension 20`, 500 sequences each, no `--against` (they are the new controls). File `yardstick/one-turn-control@a20` and `yardstick/headline-control@a20` with each file, its clears and its rules version. No control or dataset is recorded between F and V.
6. **The final round (CPU).** On the same commit as step 5: the chosen recipe's config with `"ascension": 20`, a new name (`final-a20-s<seed>`), `packs` = every Act 1 pack that loads at that commit (`content/packs/act1-*.json`; the combat packs the learner reads) plus `gen-enemies-wide.json` if the recipe is the wide one, 40k games, everything else as the recipe's config. Seed 201 first: it is the pre-registered candidate net. Seeds 1 and 101 queue after it as replicates (they measure the recipe's seed spread at A20 and never compete for promotion). Queued by 2026-11-20.
7. **Read.** Seed 201 one turn deep (`@hpb0.01@tsl1@lcb1`, trial fights `@hpb0.01@lcb1`) plus any suffix step 2 adopted, as in the step-5 control, on the headline setting at A20 `--against` the one-turn A20 control with the protocol (`yardstick/protocol`); then, on a pass, seed 201 in the product's flags (`configs/product.json` with the net swapped) at A20 against the A20 product control, 500 sequences fixed. Report by fight type (hallway, elite, boss; boss per boss) and the replicates' one-turn reads beside seed 201's. If the recipe is the wide one, its wide grid read as job 1009 (elite starts at A0; generated monsters carry no ascension rows) is descriptive, after the headline reads.

stop: go if seed 201 passes the protocol one turn deep and its product read is level or better (`decision/level-pass` bars: paired end HP - 2 se >= -1 HP, paired clears >= -0.02): promote it with `scripts/product.py set` and re-point `product/net`; fail if the one-turn read fails the protocol or the product read is below those bars: the product stays the 10-29 net, now read at A20, and the final round's nets stay as runs;
