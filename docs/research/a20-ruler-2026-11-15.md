# The A20 ruler: the Act 1 pack's hallways, and a shape that saturates (2026-11-15)

The navigator's read of probe 1020 (backlog 88 `act1-ruler`) against the A20
one-turn control 1017, and of the A20 product control 1019, both partly written
when read (1017 at 300 of 500 sequences, 1019 at 200). All three are pinned to
105691e9 (rules 334dd4b4), one-turn player `model.pt@hpb0.01@tsl1@lcb1`, trial
fights `@hpb0.01@lcb1`, headline setting (`--start starter --rewards sim
--sim-boss`) at `--ascension 20`. Real-game numbers come from
`results/real-game/*/fights.jsonl` (Act 1 floors, every A20 run of 2026-11-14,
all run rules pooled) and `docs/research/real-game-runs.md`.

## The pack's hallways cost more than the base hard pool

`--pack content/packs/act1-monsters.json` puts Gremlin Gang, Blue Slaver, Red
Slaver and the pack's Exordium Thugs (the game's version, which replaces the
base `exordium_thugs`) into the hard floors. Over 1020's 500 sequences:

| hard floors (1020) | fights | HP lost per fight | deaths |
|---|---|---|---|
| pack encounters | 619 | 14.23 | 91 (14.7%) |
| base hard encounters | 854 | 8.11 | 73 (8.5%) |

Per encounter on the first 300 sequences (HP lost per fight, net of Burning
Blood; deaths): Gremlin Gang 20.78 (1.91), 20 deaths in 92; pack Exordium Thugs
16.14 (1.61), 11 in 63, against the base Thugs' 12.77 (0.92) in 1017; Red Slaver
9.54 (0.78); Blue Slaver 6.29 (0.67). The base encounters both files share read
the same in both (large slime 11.34 / 11.53, three louse 6.42 / 6.41, looter
2.89 / 2.75), so the pack changes the pool and nothing else.

In the real game at A20 the same encounters cost as much: 21 Gremlin Gang fights
lost about 20 HP gross (about 14 net of Burning Blood), no deaths, arriving
higher; Blue Slaver with a Spike Slime 53 and Red Slaver with one 32 (two each).

Backlog 88's bar was "within 0.5 HP per fight of the base hard pool and no extra
deaths"; the pack's hallways are 6.1 HP per fight above it with 6 points more
deaths. **Go:** the A20 ruler takes the pack.

## At A20 the fixed shape is nearly unwinnable

| | clears | n |
|---|---|---|
| one turn deep, base hard pool (1017) | 0.008 | 250 |
| one turn deep, with the pack (1020) | 0.012 | 500 |
| product, two turns deep (1019) | 0.025 | 200 |
| real game, product, A20 rules 5 with the fight guard | 0.55 (18 of 33) | 33 |
| real game, A0 (rules 1) | 0.81 (22 of 27) | 27 |
| gauntlet one turn deep at A0 (`yardstick/one-turn-controls`) | 0.716 | 500 |

At A0 the gauntlet sits near the real game; at A20 it falls 20-70 times below
it. The shape (3 easy, 2 hard, elite, 2 hard, elite, hard, boss; heal 30% only
before the boss) is the cause, not the per-fight costs: a fight costs about what
it costs in the real game (Jaw Worm 3.4 net here, 8.8 gross there; Gremlin Nob
23.7 net here with deaths, 31.8 gross there), but here the fights come without a
rest in between.

Where runs end (1017, one turn deep, 250 sequences): 93 of 241 die at the first
elite, which they reach at 46 HP on average (60+ HP: 3 of 54 die; 30-44 HP: 39
of 69); 57 of 89 die at the second elite, most arriving under 30 HP; 18 of 20
die at the boss. The product (1019): first-elite arrival 52 HP, 48 of 198 die
there, 71 of 110 at the second elite, 23 of 28 at the boss. In the real game at
A20 (all rules): 54 elite fights in 63 runs (0.86 per run), arriving at 61 HP,
9 deaths (17%); bosses reached at 67 HP, 21 deaths in 51.

## What that does to the reads

Two turns deep over one turn deep, paired on the 200 sequences both files hold:

| metric | mean | sd | se | z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| end HP (HP on a clear, else 0) | +0.32 | 2.70 | 0.19 | 1.65 |
| V = clear x (0.75 x 75 + 0.25 x HP left) | +1.20 | 10.3 | 0.73 | 1.65 |
| clears | +0.020 | 0.17 | 0.012 | 1.64 |
| floors won | +0.63 | 1.69 | 0.12 | 5.28 |

At A0 the same comparison is +6.14 HP. On this shape the largest effect on
record reads +0.32 HP, so the protocol's H1 of +1.5 HP is out of reach for any
real change, and the level rule (`decision/level-pass`: end HP - 2 se >= -1 HP,
clears >= -0.02) passes almost anything, since mean end HP is under 1 HP for
both arms. The sd is small; the effect is compressed. Step 7's rule (read at n
1000 if the SPRT's pass rate at a true +2.5 HP is under 0.7) doesn't catch it:
on this shape no real change is +2.5 HP. Floors won still separates the players
(z 5.3), but it is no part of the owner's level rule.
